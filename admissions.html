Admissions

Our Streamlined Admission Process

Joining The School of Future Scientists is a seamless and supportive journey designed to welcome new students and families into our vibrant community. We encourage prospective parents and students to explore our campus and learn firsthand about the enriching educational experience we offer.

Step 1: Inquiry & Campus Visit

Begin by contacting our admissions office to schedule a personalized tour of our state-of-the-art facilities and get all your questions answered.

Step 2: Application Submission

Complete our comprehensive application form, available for download online or at our office, and submit all required documentation.

Step 3: Assessment & Interview

Prospective students will participate in an age-appropriate assessment, followed by an interview with our admissions committee to ensure a mutual fit.

Step 4: Admission Offer & Enrollment

Successful candidates will receive an official admission offer. Finalize enrollment formalities promptly to secure your child's place at SFS.

Essential Admission Requirements

To maintain our high academic standards and foster a thriving learning environment, we require the following documents and criteria for admission:

Completed Application Form

Ensuring all sections are accurately filled and signed.

Copy of Birth Certificate

Official proof of age and identity for record-keeping.

Previous Academic Records

Transcripts or report cards from the last two academic years.

Character Certificate

From the previous school, affirming good conduct and discipline.

Passport-sized Photographs

Two recent photographs for student identification.

Parent/Guardian ID Copies

Copies of CNIC or equivalent identification for legal guardians.

Specific requirements may vary based on the grade level and program. For detailed information and any additional inquiries, please do not hesitate to contact our admissions office.

Transparent Fee Structure

Our fee structure is designed to be competitive and reflective of the premium educational experience and state-of-the-art facilities we provide. We are committed to transparency and value, ensuring that our offerings are accessible to aspiring scientists.

For a detailed breakdown of tuition fees, payment schedules, and information regarding potential scholarships or financial aid options, we encourage you to contact our admissions office directly. Our team is available to discuss any financial queries you may have and assist you through the process.
