SFS Student Wins National Robotics Competition

By SFS News Desk | October 15, 2026 | Student Life

We are thrilled to announce that Ali Hassan, a brilliant student from The School of Future Scientists (SFS), has achieved an outstanding victory at the National Robotics Competition. This prestigious event, held on September 20, 2026 in Islamabad, brought together the brightest young minds from across the nation to compete in various challenging robotics categories.

Ali Hassan's innovative robot design and exceptional programming skills shone brightly throughout the competition, leading to a well-deserved first-place finish in the Advanced Robotics category. This remarkable achievement is a testament to his dedication, hard work, and the comprehensive STEM education provided at SFS.

Nurturing Future Innovators

At SFS, we are committed to fostering an environment that encourages students to explore their passions, develop critical problem-solving skills, and push the boundaries of innovation. Our robotics club and advanced science programs provide students with the resources, mentorship, and opportunities to excel in fields like robotics, engineering, and computer science.

This victory not only brings immense pride to our school but also serves as an inspiration to all our students, demonstrating what can be achieved with perseverance and a strong foundation in science and technology. We believe that such experiences are crucial in shaping the next generation of innovators and leaders.

Congratulations, Ali Hassan!

We extend our warmest congratulations to Ali Hassan on this incredible accomplishment. His success is a shining example of the caliber of talent and potential within the SFS community. We also thank our dedicated faculty for their unwavering support and guidance, which plays a pivotal role in our students' achievements.

The School of Future Scientists continues to empower students to reach for the stars and make significant contributions to the world of science and technology.
